Complete online application process

  1. Choose country and visa category: read the official immigration page and confirm the category permits your intended job.
  2. Find a real employer and vacancy: use government job portals or the employer’s own careers page; check if overseas applicants and sponsorship are accepted.
  3. Apply for the job where required: submit your CV and documents truthfully and save the application reference.
  4. Verify the offer: check employer identity, salary, hours, contract, accommodation, insurance and deductions.
  5. Employer sponsorship/approval: many routes require employer filing, labour approval or a work permit before visa application.
  6. Apply for visa: use the official government portal or embassy instructions, pay official fees and attend required interview/biometrics.
  7. Before leaving Pakistan: check BEOE/OEC requirements, recruiter licence and applicable Protector of Emigrants process.
  8. Travel only after approval: never work on a visitor visa unless expressly authorized. Keep copies of all documents.

Fraud and link safety

Never pay for a “guaranteed visa” or fake job offer. Do not send money or original passport to an unverified agent. Job portals, employer sites and immigration portals are different: a job application does not grant work authorization. If a link fails, start from the country’s main government website and navigate to immigration or labour.
